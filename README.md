# Housing-Crisis
Analytical Study of Housing Crisis in Connecticut

This project  thataims  to  analyze  the  distribution  of  affordable  housing  units  inConnecticut,  a  state  in  the  northeastern  region  of  the  UnitedStates.  The  project  uses  four  datasets,  namely,  real  estate  sales,affordable housing, total housing units, and population of townsin Connecticut. The project objectives are to analyze the afford-ability  of  housing  units  and  its  impact  on  real  estate  growth,identify  the  towns  with  the  highest  potential  for  real  estateinvestment,  and  explore  the  relationship  between  total  housingunits/affordable housing and real estate sales. The data was pre-processed  and  stored  in  a  non-relational  database,  MongoDB,and  later  in  Postgre  SQL.  The  merged  datasets  were  analyzedusing  various  Python  commands  to  visualize  and  describe  theresults. The findings indicate that affordability is higher in townswith a lower real estate sales ratio, lower population, and highertotal  housing  units.  The  project  has  significant  implications  forpolicymakers, real estate investors, and residents of Connecticut,providing  insights  into  the  housing  market’s  affordability  andpotential   investment  opportunities.   However,   the   project   hassome  limitations,  including  the  use  of  limited  datasets  and  theexclusion of some factors affecting housing affordability. Future research could focus on analyzing additional datasets and factorsinfluencing  housing  affordability.
